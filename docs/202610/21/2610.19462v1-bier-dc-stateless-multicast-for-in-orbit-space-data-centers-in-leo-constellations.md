## 摘要
新兴的数据密集型空间应用会产生大量数据，这些数据必须被高效地处理、存储、复制和交付。然而，依赖地面云区域可能使卫星数据面临较高的交付时延和时效性损失，原因在于星地可见性具有间歇性且地面基础设施接入受限。空间数据中心（SDC）将协作卫星组织为逻辑计算与存储集群，为在轨数据管理和服务连续性提供了一种有前景的方法。本文首先评估不同规模的卫星聚类如何影响卫星可见性和网关中断。然后，我们提出BIER-DC，一种面向基于卫星的SDC定制的无状态一对多通信协议。与为稳定数据中心网络架构设计的地面多播协议不同，BIER-DC将SDC间转发与SDC内投递解耦，并在数据包头中编码紧凑的簇级转发信息。与BIER、BIER-TE和Yeti的对比评估表明，BIER-DC显著降低了数据包头长度和字节跳开销，从而提升了下一代LEO空间数据中心的有效吞吐量和可扩展性。

## Abstract
Emerging data-intensive space applications generate large volumes of data that must be processed, stored, replicated, and delivered efficiently. However, relying on terrestrial cloud regions can expose satellite data to high delivery latency and loss of timeliness due to intermittent satellite-to-ground visibility and limited access to ground infrastructure. Space Data Centers (SDCs), which organize collaborative satellites into logical compute and storage clusters, offer a promising approach for in-orbit data management and service continuity. In this paper, we first evaluate how satellite clustering with different sizes affects satellite visibility and gateway outage. We then propose BIER-DC, a stateless one-to-many communication protocol tailored for satellite-based SDCs. Unlike terrestrial multicast protocols designed for stable data center fabrics, BIER-DC decouples inter-SDC forwarding from intra-SDC delivery and encodes compact cluster-level forwarding information in packet headers. Comparative evaluations against BIER, BIER-TE, and Yeti show that BIER-DC significantly reduces packet-header length and byte-hop overhead, improving effective goodput and scalability for next-generation LEO space data centers.
