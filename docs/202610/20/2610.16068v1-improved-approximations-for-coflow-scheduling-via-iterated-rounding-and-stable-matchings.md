## 摘要
我们研究由 Chowdhury、Zhong 和 Stoica（2014）提出的 Coflow 调度问题。我们给出了确定性多项式时间算法：在无释放时间时达到 2.77-近似，在整数释放时间下达到 3.17-近似。这些结果改进了 Rohwedder 和 Schnaars（2025）的 3.42 和 4.36 保证。我们的方法结合了两个思想：（1）一种新颖的迭代舍入方案；（2）Galvin 的列表边染色方法，该方法基于 Gale–Shapley 稳定匹配算法。

## Abstract
We study Coflow Scheduling, introduced by Chowdhury, Zhong, and Stoica (2014). We give deterministic polynomial-time algorithms achieving a $2.77$-approximation without release dates and a $3.17$-approximation with integer release dates. These improve the $3.42$ and $4.36$ guarantees of Rohwedder and Schnaars (2025).   Our approach combines two ideas: (1) a novel iterated rounding scheme, and (2) Galvin's list edge coloring method, which is based on the Gale--Shapley stable matching algorithm.
