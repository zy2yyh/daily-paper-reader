## 摘要
光电路交换机（OCS）使AI集群中的pod间布线可编程，由此带来布线选择、对称性偏离与重配置等设计问题。我们提出一个统一的信息几何框架来解决这些问题。一种布线是度多面体内线路上的概率单纯形中的一个点；与对称Fat-Tree先验q的偏离由DKL(· || q)度量。代价U为平滑后的DAG关键路径长度，其梯度通过前向–后向递推等于某个Gibbs分布的路径边缘分布。对于自由能F_{β_1} = U + β_1^{-1} DKL(· || q)，我们证明了其适定性、对β_1的单调依赖性、基于Jeffreys散度的自适应界，以及静态最优性的对称性原理。一种熵近端梯度方法通过|V|维对偶KL投影进行乘性更新，并实现线性收敛。对于时变流量，阈值策略相对于无切换最优解产生O(√(ΓC V_1 T))的遗憾。流量变化由具有非渐近虚警界的多项GLR–CUSUM检测器检测。多作业广义均值公平性与α-公平性（α≥2）相一致。在流水线、数据和MoE工作负载上的实验以整数规划为基准验证了该框架。

## Abstract
Optical Circuit Switches (OCS) make inter-pod wiring in AI clusters programmable, posing design questions on wiring choice, symmetry departure, and reconfigurations. We present a unified information-geometric framework to address these issues.A wiring is a point in a probability simplex over circuits within a degree polytope. Departure from a symmetric Fat-Tree prior $\mathbf{q}$ is measured by $\DKL(\cdot \,\|\, \mathbf{q})$. The cost $U$ is the smoothed DAG critical-path length, whose gradient equals path marginals of a Gibbs distribution via forward--backward recursion.For free energy $F_{β_1} = U + β_1^{-1} \DKL(\cdot \,\|\, \mathbf{q})$, we prove well-posedness, monotonic dependence on $β_1$, adaptation bounds via Jeffreys divergence, and a symmetry principle for static optimality. An entropic proximal-gradient method updates multiplicatively with a $|V|$-dim dual KL projection, achieving linear convergence.For time-varying traffic, a threshold policy yields regret $O(\sqrt{ΓC V_1 T})$ against the switching-free optimum. Traffic changes are detected by a multinomial GLR--CUSUM detector with non-asymptotic false-alarm bounds. Multi-job generalized-mean fairness aligns with $α$-fairness ($α\ge 2$). Experiments on pipeline, data, and MoE workloads validate the framework against integer programming.
