## 摘要
在电话模型中，一条消息从源顶点开始，每一轮中每个已获知信息的顶点可以呼叫一个邻居。电话多播要求给出一个调度方案，以最少的轮数通知给定的 $k$ 个终端。在有向图上，该问题此前已知最好的近似比为 $\tilde O(\sqrt k)$，由 Elkin 和 Kortsarz 以及 Hathcock、Kortsarz 和 Ravi 给出。对于每个整数 $\ell\ge2$，我们给出一个确定性组合算法，其运行时间为 $n^{O(\ell)}$，可计算出一个长度为 $O(\ell^3\log k)\cdot(\mathrm{OPT}+k^{1/\ell})$ 的调度，其中 $n$ 是顶点数，$\mathrm{OPT}$ 是最优值。由于 $\mathrm{OPT}\ge\log_2(k+1)$，对于每个固定的 $\varepsilon>0$，该算法在时间 $n^{O(1/\varepsilon)}$ 内给出近似比 $O(k^\varepsilon)$，并在拟多项式时间内给出近似比 $O(\log^4k)$。该方法基于带可加顶点负载的跳跃树、限制每个顶点负载的高度约简，以及具有指数势函数的递归贪心算法。我们还证明，有向多播是时间展开有向图上度约束有向 Steiner 树问题的一个特例。利用该问题的已知算法，可得到运行时间为 $n^{O(\log n)}$ 的随机化近似比 $O(\log^2n)$，以及运行时间为 $n^{O(1/\varepsilon)}$ 的近似比 $O(n^\varepsilon)$；与我们的结果不同，这些近似比会随 $n$ 增长。在困难性方面，即使 $\mathrm{OPT}=O(\log k)$，有向多播仍然是 $(1-\varepsilon)\ln k$-困难的，而这正是此前算法表现最弱的情形。

## Abstract
In the telephone model a message starts at a source vertex, and in every round each informed vertex may call one neighbour. Telephone multicast asks for a schedule that informs a given set of $k$ terminals in the fewest rounds. On directed graphs, the best known ratio for this problem was $\tilde O(\sqrt k)$, due to Elkin and Kortsarz and to Hathcock, Kortsarz and Ravi.   For every integer $\ell\ge2$, we give a deterministic combinatorial algorithm with running time $n^{O(\ell)}$ that computes a schedule of length $O(\ell^3\log k)\cdot(\mathrm{OPT}+k^{1/\ell})$, where $n$ is the number of vertices and $\mathrm{OPT}$ the optimum. Since $\mathrm{OPT}\ge\log_2(k+1)$, this gives ratio $O(k^ε)$ in time $n^{O(1/ε)}$ for every fixed $ε>0$, and ratio $O(\log^4k)$ in quasi-polynomial time. The method rests on jump trees with additive vertex loads, a height reduction that bounds the load of every vertex, and a recursive greedy algorithm with an exponential potential. We also show that directed multicast is a special case of the degree-bounded directed Steiner tree problem on a time-expanded digraph. With known algorithms for that problem this gives randomized ratios $O(\log^2n)$ in time $n^{O(\log n)}$ and $O(n^ε)$ in time $n^{O(1/ε)}$, which, unlike ours, grow with $n$.   On the hardness side, directed multicast stays $(1-ε)\ln k$-hard even when $\mathrm{OPT}=O(\log k)$, the regime in which the previous algorithms were weakest.
