## 摘要
稀疏激活的混合专家（MoE）模型的分布式训练常常受限于动态 token 路由所引起的工作负载不均衡和全对全（A2A）通信。现有的 token 去重、专家放置和复制技术可以缓解这些瓶颈，但未能联合刻画它们各自不同的负载语义。在分层 token 去重下，通信在每个层级上对每个目的组中的每个 token 只计数一次，而专家计算会处理每一个 token-专家分配。采用复制时，这两类开销共同取决于物理布局和 token 到副本的映射，因此分别优化是次优的。我们提出 PlaceMoE 来解决这种耦合问题。首先，我们形式化了在分布于 P 个设备上的 E 个专家场景中专家布局与源感知 token 到副本映射的联合优化，同时考虑分层通信流量和专家计算负载。其次，我们开发了一个求解器，结合了协同选择感知的专家分组、用于副本分配的有界动态规划，以及布局与映射的交替细化。第三，PlaceMoE 将副本梯度同步与注意力反向传播重叠。我们在 32-GPU 集群和 64-NPU 集群上使用 Qwen3-VL 和 DeepSeek-V3 评估了 PlaceMoE。实验结果表明，与最先进的训练框架 VeOmni 相比，PlaceMoE 在 A2A 通信上实现了最高 6.94 倍的加速，在端到端训练上实现了 1.74-2.33 倍的加速。

## Abstract
Distributed training of sparsely activated mixture-of-experts (MoE) models is often bottlenecked by workload imbalance and all-to-all (A2A) communication induced by dynamic token routing. Existing token deduplication, expert placement, and replication techniques alleviate these bottlenecks but do not jointly capture their distinct load semantics. Under hierarchical token deduplication, communication counts each token once per destination group at each hierarchy level, whereas expert computation processes every token-expert assignment. With replication, both costs depend jointly on physical layout and token-to-copy mapping, making separate optimization suboptimal. We present PlaceMoE to address this coupling. First, we formulate the joint optimization of expert layout and source-aware token-to-copy mapping for E experts distributed across P devices, accounting for both hierarchical communication traffic and expert-compute load. Second, we develop a solver that combines co-selection-aware expert grouping, bounded dynamic programming for replica allocation, and alternating refinement of layout and mapping. Third, PlaceMoE overlaps replica-gradient synchronization with attention backward. We evaluate PlaceMoE on a 32-GPU cluster and a 64-NPU cluster using Qwen3-VL and DeepSeek-V3. Experimental results show that PlaceMoE achieves up to a 6.94x speedup in A2A communication and 1.74-2.33x speedups in end-to-end training over the state-of-the-art training framework VeOmni.
